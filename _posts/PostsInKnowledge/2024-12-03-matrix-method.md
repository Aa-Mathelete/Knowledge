## What is it?
Based on what I think are important to note down in most classes, I invented a way of taking notes. This method, named *The Matrix Method*, shows four **main** 'matrices'(Or Boxes). Here is what each box is used for:
-<div class="thi-columns" markdown="1">
- **Terminology**: This box is used to write down important definitions for the concept you are learning.
- **The Notetaking Body**: This one is self-explanatory, you can write your outline method style notes here.
- **Visuals**: This one is also self-expanatory, draw any visuals to aid you here. Some examples include mindmaps, maps, graphs, or some simple drawings.
- **Free Space**: Also self-explanatory, use this areas for doodles, extra space, or more visuals--use this space for your needs.
</div>
