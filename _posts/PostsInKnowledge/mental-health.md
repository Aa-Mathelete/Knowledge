*You're drowning...again*


 Academics, personal life, your goals, family,...balance is a tricky thing and you seem to be losing your grip on keeping it. We all feel it, and it starts of with feeling the stress of all the things you do and want to. It could be big goals like working on your research paper, to smaller ones like finishing your homework. We all have felt it before, you want to do everything in the momment of now: take a break, learn, finish your homework, spend time with your family... 

 ## How to Find Balance
People lose their balance when they have a lot on their plate and are just staring back at it. Another way of putting it, it's like taking more food than you can really eat. From my experience, to regain my balance on this balance beam named life, you have to go step by step, chew by chew. 
<img src="https://bloximages.newyork1.vip.townnews.com/nola.com/content/tncms/assets/v3/editorial/1/f2/1f2111a6-e307-11ee-a4f3-df85a31dd618/65f4b030b50e7.preview.jpg?crop=1096%2C1096%2C397%2C0&resize=1200%2C1200&order=crop%2Cresize" alt="Don't Want To Eat This In One Go" width="500" height="600">

When steping back on, use techiniques like the Eisenhower Matrix(Sorting things by Priority), Pomodoro(Creating time segments of 25 minitues to get work done) or by making a simple calender (Personally, I like using a mix of them all). Remember to go step by step, your goal, and take breaks between(Don't over work yourself!).
